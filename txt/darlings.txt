    Quantum computing and machine learning are currently at
    the peak of computer scientists' attention. It comes as no surprise that
    their natural intersection --- quantum machine learning  (QML) ---  also is. 
    
    
    Quantum, or more often hybrid quantum-classical, models consists
	of so-called quantum layers. Those kind of layers, coming in various
	shapes and sizes, describes the quantum computational part of the model
	data processing pipeline. However, hybrid models are only one branch
	of QML --- the other are quantum methods of learning.
